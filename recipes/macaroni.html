Macaroni

Description

Macaroni is a type of pasta that is commonly used in various dishes.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of macaroni
  • 2 tablespoons of butter
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1 cup of shredded cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Preparation Steps

  1. Cook the macaroni according to the package instructions.
  2. In a saucepan, melt the butter and add the milk.
  3. Stir in the cheese until it's fully melted and combined.
  4. Drain the cooked macaroni and mix it with the cheese sauce.
  5. Serve hot and enjoy!

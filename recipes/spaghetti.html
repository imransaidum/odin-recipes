Spaghetti

Description

Spaghetti is a type of pasta that is commonly used in various dishes.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of spaghetti
  • 2 tablespoons of olive oil
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 1 cup of tomato sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Preparation Steps

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
  2. In a saucepan, heat the olive oil and sauté the garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the tomato sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Drain the cooked spaghetti and mix it with the tomato sauce.
  5. Serve hot and enjoy!

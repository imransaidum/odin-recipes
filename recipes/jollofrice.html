Jollof Rice

Description

Jollof Rice is a popular West African dish made with rice,

tomatoes, and a variety of spices.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of rice
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 teaspoon of curry powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Preparation Steps

  1. Rinse the rice and set it aside.
  2. Dice the tomatoes and onion.
  3. Chop the garlic.
  4. In a pot, heat some oil and sauté the onion until translucent.
  5. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
  6. Stir in the curry powder and cook for 30 seconds.
  7. Add the tomatoes and cook until they break down.
  8. Add the rice and stir to combine.
  9. Pour in water or broth and bring to a boil.
  10. Reduce heat and simmer until the rice is cooked through.
